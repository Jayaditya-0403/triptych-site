How it works

One screen. Three panels.

  1. I

    Swipe

    Each panel moves on its own. Change the top and your jeans and shoes stay where they are, so you compare one piece at a time.

  2. II

    Stack

    Filter by department and occasion, then choose what feeds each panel: jeans, trousers or shorts under the top, or a dress for a full look.

  3. III

    Slay

    The bar at the bottom keeps a running total. Put all three pieces in your bag at once, open any of them on the retailer's site, or try the outfit on with AI.

In the studio

Around the three panels

The panels do the styling. Everything else sits within a thumb's reach.

  • Colour and size

    Pick a colourway and the picture changes to match. Sizes that have sold out are struck through before you choose.
